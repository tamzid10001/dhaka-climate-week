About 01 / 07

What is Dhaka Climate Week

Dhaka is often described through the lens of risk. We see something different. We see a city that continues to grow, adapt, and innovate despite some of the world's most complex climate challenges. Dhaka Climate Week exists because the future of climate action will not be built only in wealthy capitals. It will also be built in cities like Dhaka, where resilience is practiced every day and where the Global South is shaping solutions for the world.
