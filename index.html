About 01 — 05

What is Dhaka Climate Week

2027 First edition · Dhaka

Dhaka is often described through the lens of risk. We see something different. We see a city that continues to grow, adapt, and innovate despite some of the world's most complex climate challenges. Dhaka Climate Week exists because the future of climate action will not be built only in wealthy capitals. It will also be built in cities like Dhaka, where resilience is practiced every day and where the Global South is shaping solutions for the world.

Why Dhaka?

Bangladesh carries lived experience across nearly every major climate challenge. Dhaka Climate Week reframes that experience as exportable, practical knowledge not a cautionary tale.

Why Global South

Fast-growing economies and the greatest climate exposure both sit in the Global South yet no single platform exists for peer-to-peer, implementation-focused exchange.

South Asia ⇄ ASEAN

Bangladesh sits geographically in South Asia, and economically and culturally close to ASEAN, a rare natural connector few other cities can credibly claim.

The crisis is already here. The delta doesn't wait.
