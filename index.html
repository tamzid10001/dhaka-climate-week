About

What is Dhaka Climate Week

01

Climate Week connects cities worldwide to accelerate action. New York, London, Medellín — now Dhaka.

Five days of conferences, labs, and city-wide events. The world's most climate-exposed megacity as a platform for encounter and action.

Why Dhaka

Built on
Resilience

Twenty million residents. Rising seas. Extreme monsoons. A megacity at the heart of the delta — where urgency and ingenuity meet every day.

From Korail to Hatirjheel, Dhaka's neighborhoods are living laboratories for urban climate adaptation.

The crisis is already here. The delta doesn't wait.
