Six Masterclass & Panel sessions are planned for Day 1 of Dhaka Climate Week. Choose which session(s) to co-design, support with experts, and communicate to your networks — tick your selections below and submit.

How partners can get involved

✓ Co-Design the Session

Work with the DCW team to shape the agenda, speakers, and format of the session.

Your commitment 1–2 planning calls, content input, speaker suggestions

✓ Support with a High-Profile Expert

Nominate or fund a globally recognized speaker, panelist, or keynote voice for the session.

Your commitment Identify & confirm speaker, coordinate logistics

✓ Communicate with Participants

Co-brand outreach to your network — members, employees, communities — to drive attendance.

Your commitment 2–3 co-branded emails or social posts to your audience

Partners may choose one or more scopes per session. Multiple partners can collaborate on the same session.

✓Co-Design = shape the session agenda ✓Expert = provide one high-profile speaker ✓Communicate = outreach to your network
